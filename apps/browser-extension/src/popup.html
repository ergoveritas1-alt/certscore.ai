What this scan collects

After you start the scan, CertScore.ai collects the target URL, bounded request metadata, cookie names and attributes, visible consent-interface evidence, linked privacy/legal surface URLs and bounded visible policy text, transport/form/embed metadata, and a visible-tab screenshot. The extension requests temporary access to linked sites so those surfaces can be inspected. Cookie values, form entries, passwords, and payment information are not captured, and consent controls are not clicked.

Evidence is sent securely to CertScore.ai only to create your scan report.
