## LinkedIn

We looked at 5,000 recent CertScore.ai production scans.

Among 3,199 distinct domains with usable evidence, we observed session-replay service signals on 383 — 12.0%.

Microsoft Clarity was the most commonly observed service, appearing on 265 of the 383 replay-positive domains (69.2%). That describes our sample, not worldwide market share.

CertScore.ai helps teams identify these signals and review the retained evidence. A service signal can include a library download; it does not prove that visitor sessions or inputs were recorded.
