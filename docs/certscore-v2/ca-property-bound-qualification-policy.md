# CertScore.ai property-bound California collection qualification

Owner authorized local implementation on October 22, 2026. No deployment or
historical report rewrite is authorized. Existing scan budgets and passive CMP
restrictions remain unchanged.

## Minimum bar

New `gpc_sale_sharing_evidence.v5` / `california-gpc-response.v10-property-bound`
and `california-post-reject.v3-property-bound` assessments may join an explicit,
verified, target-owned property-purpose declaration with successful identified
collection for that exact property in the relevant choice session. A backend
account link, cookie equality, and direct observation of an audience export are
not additional prerequisites for this corroborated risk signal.

The initial deterministic recipes support English policy disclosures that name
an exact `G-…` Analytics property and explicitly state sale to Google for valuable
consideration, sharing with Google for cross-context behavioral advertising,
remarketing or retargeting, or use of that property for those advertising/audience
purposes. Example, modal, negated, ambiguous, generic vendor, unsupported language
and contradictory passages do not qualify. These are bounded, closed recipes;
they are not comprehensive natural-language interpretation. A bare property
configuration, default feature setting, ordinary Analytics traffic, or adjacent
Ads script alone remains insufficient.

The source must be a verified original same-scan/same-target policy bundle, with
usable target-controller/first-party ownership at confidence >=0.9, successful
retrieval and policy-document role. Inspect at most eight existing observations,
6,000 characters and 100 clauses per observation; retain at most eight positive
declarations. Retain source-bundle, document, excerpt, clause, property and
request digests plus a bounded declaration excerpt. No fetch or model call is
added. Static policy purpose may corroborate another lane's request; identifiers,
request execution, GPC delivery, restrictions and choice timing remain in their
own session. Later CMP or queue state never supplies an earlier choice.

GPC preserves its original verified document, signal delivery, canonical endpoint
attribution, exact retained request and successful response gates. An exact
property plus valid `cid` can be inspected from the URL or an already-retained,
complete same-Request body/header proof. Missing supplemental proof does not
veto an independently qualifying URL. The existing 100-qualification cap, 48
capture inspections and 32 detailed proofs remain unchanged.

Reject retains authorized completed click, source/target/document/ancestry,
observation window, successful response and complete required request proof.
New capture retains a property digest alongside valid identified collection;
historical packets without that binding cannot borrow it from another session.

## Processing and canonical scoring

NPA alone is context, not an automatic exemption. Other supported request-local
restrictions and invalid/conflicting privacy declarations remain neutral under
the existing policy. Independent qualifying sibling requests remain eligible.
The typed proof is persisted before normalized concerns, concern policy, unified
findings and scoring. Recipients are unioned across GPC, Reject and explicit
opt-out once under the shared 47-point family. EU Reject presence scoring and
the separate five-point GPP family are unchanged.

Historical GPC v1–v4 qualifiers retain their policy mappings, including v4 -> v9.
Stored California Reject v1/v2 assessments are revalidated under their stored
version, without the new declaration join or diagnostic changes. Customer
reports show factual findings, evidence and scores, without deduction formulas.

## Diagnostics and cost

New Reject diagnostics independently identify retained collection metadata,
property binding and supported purpose. Retained Analytics measurement evidence
is not described as missing request metadata when purpose is unresolved. Unknown
purpose is not absence, honoring, or proof of a legal violation.

No Options panel, extra browser work, lane, invocation, wait, retry, worker size,
model call, paid API call or retention-period change is added. Bounded positive
proof metadata is estimated below $0.25/month at 100,000 scans with 30-day
retention; this below-$1 increase was disclosed before implementation. Existing
worker bytes are reused for the policy join; no extra S3 read is added.
