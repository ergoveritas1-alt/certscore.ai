# CertScore.ai local opt-out verification hardening

The owner approved local opt-out verification hardening on October 25, 2026,
including InspiredTaste, without score effects. This approval authorizes no
new website capture, scan-model/provider call, deployment, flag change or
historical rewrite. The combined cost estimate must remain within $0.50/month.

The new `california.request-restriction-shadow.v2` is an optional local observer
result. The normal materializer retains the unchanged v1 analysis and consumes
that completed basis for registration before any refinement. Registration
materialization never consumes the new research result or its limitations.
There is no additional canonical finding, concern, score rule, persisted field,
evidence box or top finding for opt-out verification. Frequency analysis stays
unchanged and inactive for deductions.

## Evidence and scope

The helper consumes only existing verified, complete, owned, untranslated
English sources admitted by the California projection. Keep the existing
4,000-character preceding California scope and intervening-jurisdiction gate.
No source read or excerpt-to-complete promotion is added. Fixed reviewed
grammars support the original once-confirm-verifiable-request condition,
explicit identity-verification-before-processing wording and an explicit
above-rights email procedure. The latter requires substantive enumerated
sale/sharing opt-out wording within the same bounded preceding scope; a heading,
generic rights list or nearby verification sentence alone does not suffice.

InspiredTaste's retained California supplement expressly enumerates sale and
sharing opt-out rights, then states that any of those rights requires a
verifiable consumer request and identity verification through its email route.
This supports a statement about that route's policy wording. Separate browser,
cookie or industry choices do not establish that the email condition disappears,
and the research result does not claim all routes are blocked or a request was
actually refused. Ambiguous Remotive, UFC and Daily.dev procedures stay held.
Showit's ADMT wording and Devtodev's future-sale condition remain outside this
sale/sharing candidate.

Explicit identity-verification exceptions are checked across the entire bounded
source in chunks, including reverse wording and exceptions outside the quote
window. Historical, example, third-party attribution, reversed polarity and
conditional fraud context remain conservative holds. Holding conditional fraud
wording does not establish that every verification demand in fraud handling is
lawful. Unsupported semantics remain unknown; this is not a general legal
language classifier or a claim of population precision/recall.

## Budgets and isolation

The observer shares the original absolute 10 ms cooperative allowance and the
remaining slots in the original 32-candidate budget. It uses at most eight
documents of 160,000 characters. Contradiction checks scan each document once
in bounded chunks rather than scanning a whole document for each candidate.
No opt-out row is emitted until the contradiction scan finishes. Each exact
UTF-16 quote range is at most 600 characters, context at most 8,000, and the
inclusive serialized observer output remains at most 2,048 UTF-8 bytes.

Timeout, candidate exhaustion or an extension error cannot alter the saved v1
registration basis. Non-opt-out fact vectors stay unchanged. Output pressure
drops the new opt-out result first. If additional limitation metadata cannot
fit alongside the original non-opt-out facts, the helper returns a compatible
v1 view with the opt-out row removed; that fallback makes no v2 conclusion.
Absence of a research row is never evidence that no condition exists.

## Cost and validation

The helper executes only when the existing local observer is supplied. Normal
materializations add no analysis pass, packet field, persistence operation or
research quote retention. The prior conditional planning model therefore stays
approximately $0.476/month at 100,000 scans, rather than adding the previously
illustrated $0.17 storage scenario. Its existing 30-day retention, 20 KiB growth
allowance, effective database rate and compute assumptions still apply; this is
not a current AWS price quote or a universal payload bound. There is no worker
memory/capacity change. Actual local work adds no recurring service cost.

Independent Astra review and the original-receipt 571-site before/after replay
are retained under `artifacts/ca-optout-hardening-2026-10-25/`. They compare normal
and observed materializations, actual canonical shared checklist rows, normalized
and unified findings, CA/EU scores and source exclusions. Dedicated controls
cover dense opt-out anchors before registration, expired/mid-document budgets,
distant exceptions and output pressure as well as semantic cases.

seven-test hardener rerun, both TypeScript checks and diff validation. Independent
Astra passed 45 semantic/retained-source controls and four budget/isolation
