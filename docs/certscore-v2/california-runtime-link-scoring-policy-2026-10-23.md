## Qualification

The verified projector may retain a runtime proof only when all of the following hold:

- The original representative starting page has a current complete named-choice inspection, no named choice, and the existing source/document/frame/absence guarantees.
- The independently verified typed GPC observation belongs to the same scan and exact starting-page URL. Independent sessions retain their separate hashes; URL agreement never proves a shared DOM or browser document.
- Main-document header and navigator delivery are verified and the current approved `california-gpc-response.v10-property-bound` policy identifies successful qualifying collection. The new basis does not use comparison-only GPC, an unknown CMP state, a bare acknowledgment/contradiction, ordinary analytics, scripts, failed collection, unsupported signals or restricted processing.
- Request references and canonical entity IDs match the current qualifying GPC inventory. The proof is bounded to 32 requests and retains the original inspection hash plus GPC source, session and document hashes.

For this positive runtime basis, a declaration of California sale/sharing and complete English policy text are unnecessary. No missing-policy conclusion is inferred. A contrary no-sale policy claim remains retained wording and cannot erase the independent runtime observation; privacy-choice coverage stays reviewed rather than being labeled not triggered.

## Canonical scoring

The assessment is persisted before normalized concern -> concern policy -> reportable unified finding -> canonical score. Concern policy receives the already-retained typed GPC assessment as transient context; it does not duplicate that assessment in the new persisted proof or add a display shortcut.

Both concern policy and scoring re-join the proof to the GPC evidence. Final scoring additionally requires a separately surfaced, reportable, eligible GPC finding with its exact canonical score effect. A stored proof or Boolean alone is insufficient. Missing, suppressed, mismatched or unsupported sibling evidence leaves the link effect neutral while preserving independently eligible findings.

The link-access effect is six points once per scan, alongside the existing GPC family. It does not add recipient entities, multiply per request/link, change the shared 47-point collection cap or add another effect when the declaration basis also qualifies. Historical source markers never acquire the runtime effect on read. Customer snapshot wording, finding copy, detailed evidence and the prohibition on customer deduction explanations remain in place.
