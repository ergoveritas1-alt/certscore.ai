# Passive CMP inventory for CertScore.ai

The owner requires CMP detail capture to remain passive as of October 21, 2026.
This supersedes earlier Options-opening recipes for newly executed CMP preference
workers. Preserve historical evidence and assessments on read.

The existing optional worker captures the starting document, visible controls,
explicit service names and declarations from already-loaded canonical provider
APIs, visible inventory and pre-choice privacy-link hit tests. It must not open
Options/Manage, call SDK show/open functions, expand panels, follow preference
links, submit forms, toggle choices, scroll to reveal panels or run panel-only
accessibility work. Accept and Reject observation lanes retain their separate
existing authorization. No rollout flag is enabled by this implementation.

New packets retain `captureMode: passive_cmp_inventory.v1` and producer revision
`cmp_preferences.v19`. The historical action intent field remains unchanged;
count is zero and opened is false. Bound checkpoint timestamps describe actual
completed reads. A completed passive visit does not establish full preference
inventory, feature absence, visible disclosure of hidden configuration, semantic
registration or legal compliance. Missing/ambiguous/broken sources stay unknown
or limited. Deeper preference sections remain explicitly uninspected.

Preserve the current invocation, memory/GB-second policy, absolute deadline,
cleanup allowance, retention period and single-result publication. Read available
inventory before slower link inspection. At most the existing bounded readiness
checkpoint may supply late data; no navigation retry, new browser session, model
call, fetch or longer wait is introduced. Existing consent-proof passive inventory
remains loader-bound to its own lane.

For a degraded representative consent visit, a positive observed surface plus
verified complete structured geometry and matching observation/DOM/geometry
loader tokens may preserve its primary document. Access must remain loaded,
unchallenged and below HTTP 400. Failed, empty, blocked, incomplete-structure or
mismatched captures cannot use this exception. It does not turn covered controls
into actionable controls or assert absence. New materializations use projector
2.2.4; historical persisted conclusions remain unchanged.

Unnamed observed cookies retain exact source references rather than fabricated
names or name-matching inputs. The v7 CMP assessment supports those references.
An optional CMP schema failure must not prevent independent verified evidence
from completing the canonical result. No finding, score rule or customer scoring
explanation is added.

All facts follow verified packet -> typed persisted assessment -> normalized
concern -> concern policy -> unified/checklist -> shared report/API projection.
Replay is internal and does not rewrite production reports. No deployment is
authorized. Removing panel work reduces optional execution; bounded additional
mode/version/category metadata is estimated below $0.10/month at 100,000 scans,
within the previously disclosed sub-$0.50 metadata estimate. No infrastructure,
retention, model or scan-budget increase is introduced.

## Local verification

The controlled replay of all 122 October 21 retained scans verified 244 original
bundle/manifest files and 122 geometry files. All 122 candidate parent assessments
validate without projection errors. No live public scan or production write ran.

Across 1,586 CMP check rows, Not assessed falls from 674 to 654; Incomplete rises
from 545 to 548. Eleven rows become Observed and six become Partially assessed,
resolving 17 previously unresolved checks: seven on Salomon and ten additional
checks in MyCWT's previously unavailable report. Three Salomon rows refine to
Incomplete and are not counted as resolved. No existing Incomplete row becomes
assessed. In the stable original 28 named-CMP scans, unresolved rows fall from
188 to 181. Future passive-capture improvements are not counted as historical
replay recoveries. Detailed local artifacts are in
`artifacts/cmp-passive-fixes-2026-10-21/`.

Validation: 282 focused CMP/consent tests pass, including passive action traps,
legacy contract compatibility, document drift, optional projection isolation and
shared report projection. Contracts, scan-core, API contracts, SDK and web
typechecks pass; generated API/SDK contract parity passes.
