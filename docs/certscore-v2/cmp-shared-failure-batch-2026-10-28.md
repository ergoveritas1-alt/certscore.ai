## Evidence and ranking

`scripts/cmp-retained-failure-sweep.ts` consumes an explicit manifest of original
packet paths, hashes, scan/action identities and separate cohort labels. It
validates packet bytes, schema, parent scan identity and expected historical
registration before canonical projection. Duplicate scan/action identities,
missing contacted packets and hash drift fail closed. Outputs require a new
folder. Historical, reference, candidate and targeted follow-up cohorts remain
separate; no selected-cohort rate is presented as population reliability.

The reviewed month archive contains 5,026 Mac mini scans / 10,052 action rows,
with all 5,026 original source hashes checked. The available original packet
subset contains 1,466 actions across 768 sites; it is not the entire archive.
There are 1,016 unresolved-control actions across 573 sites in that subset and
194 navigation/authorization outcomes across 104 sites. Legacy unknowns are not
proof that the site lacks a CMP/control or that a specific scanner bug occurred.

In the fixed 76-contact candidate benchmark, the three largest unresolved stage
groups were control resolution (32 actions / 22 sites), category/source binding
(19 / 11), and receipt protocol (7 / 4). These groups select the workstreams;
they do not establish that every row has the same cause or has now been repaired.
Stage groups are exclusive; secondary diagnostic exposures overlap. A confirmed
decision with incomplete observation remains a capture-completion limitation.

## CookieYes renewal metadata

The retained CookieYes writer appends `lastRenewedDate` as a renewal epoch, outside
its category map. The canonical decoder supports this exact field as a bounded
13-digit value. Unknown fields/categories, duplicate fields, invalid values and
incomplete category inventories remain unverified. This applies to both actions
through the existing shared decoder.

An internal change fingerprint excludes renewal metadata and field order, so a
renewal-only change cannot become fresh decision proof. Original raw-value hashes
still bind evidence and witnesses; the internal fingerprint is never substituted
into retained evidence. Existing source/control authorization, isolated sessions,
changed receipt, decision direction, write timestamp and readback checks remain.
No current or historical receipt is rewritten. Hash-only historical records do
not gain reconstructed raw values or newly confirmed registrations.

The diagnostic visits also observed Complianz split-cookie and WPConsent JSON
receipts. They do not justify guessing a complete configured category universe.
Those adapters remain open, without a generic boolean/all-consent shortcut.
Diagnostic receipt-shape logging deduplicated equal values across keys; therefore
that shape log alone cannot establish which Complianz category keys were absent.
Original packets retain their own storage identities and hashes unchanged.
