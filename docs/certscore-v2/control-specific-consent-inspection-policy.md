# Definitive first-layer consent controls

Owner-approved implementation, September 14, 2026. This extends structured control evidence 2.1 with assessment **2.2**, capture policy **consent-control-capture.v3**, label registry **v4**, and **control_specific_inspection.v1**. Historical assessments keep their stored contract and conclusions.

## Meaning and evidence requirements

- **Observed:** a visible first-layer control of this type was retained. This does not confirm that clicking it registered consent or refusal.
- **Not observed:** complete inspection relevant to this control retained no visible control of that type. This is scoped to the captured initial layer; deeper refusal availability and finding eligibility remain separate.
- **Inspection limited:** a specific capture or semantic limitation prevents the affected answer. Preserve machine-readable `unknown`. Reports show one inspection notice alongside known binary answers.

New per-control absence requires the verified retained geometry packet, matching canonical loader token and document, a usable consent session, and structurally complete capture. The packet retains candidate count, unique candidate identities, structural roles and unresolved intents, overflow status, main-document readiness, frame counts and stability. Validation checks exact relevant-candidate coverage and canonical role consistency; malformed or contradictory proof cannot establish absence. A complete empty inventory explicitly retains count zero and completed capture coverage, and continues to require the established completed whole-surface inspection path. The new per-control path requires a nonempty relevant control inventory and a geometry timestamp at least as recent as the latest typed consent observation. Missing inventory and early/stale empty geometry cannot establish absence.

An unresolved Options information affordance can leave Options limited while Reject is `not_observed`. An unlabeled, untranslated or conflicting possible decision control limits all affected intents. Canonical consent-classifier structural partner-list patterns and policy links do not imply refusal and do not block an otherwise complete Reject inspection. Inaccessible frames, truncation, navigation/document drift and access failures cannot be converted to absence. A union of recaptures does not borrow one capture's per-control proof; existing verified whole-inventory assessment remains available.

The overall assessment remains limited when any required control is unresolved. A complete assessment still contains binary controls. The new per-control proof cannot override a no-go or document mismatch, synthesize action evidence, or substitute for source-pointer checksum verification in canonical materialization.

## Recognition changes

Observation-only reviewed phrases recover English and German necessary-only controls, German “Nein Danke,” Dutch “Alles afwijzen,” and Russian refusal/approval. Visible and accessible labels are classified separately; a nonconflicting accessibility description no longer destroys an exact visible match. Opposite decisions, negations and informational references still veto the classification. Existing action classification and named-CMP action recipes are unchanged.

The initial selected-only recipe is restricted to Drupal EU Cookie Compliance's exact first-layer category-submit structure, a unique native submit button with a reviewed save-selection label, and a complete visible checkbox inventory. The mandatory category must be checked and disabled; at least one optional category must exist and every optional category must be unchecked and enabled. Hidden/missing/ambiguous categories, conflicting labels and optional-on defaults prevent credit. The retained typed category facts travel from the verified geometry packet into assessment evidence and its source hash. They are not copied into the bundle’s simpler control rows as unqualified Reject labels. This observation does not authorize clicking Save or traversing settings.

## Canonical projection

Retained scanner evidence → WC01 2.2 assessment → unchanged typed persistence → normalized consent inventory and absence eligibility → concern policy → checklist/findings/score → report and export.

Each missing-control concern must use verified absence for its own control. The canonical guard suppresses reject-missing and absence-based prominence claims when the typed Reject result is unknown, malformed, mismatched or blocked. Positive visual comparison remains separate. Known per-control absence must not be reset to unknown merely because another control remains limited. Paid-decline and dismissal interpretations retain their own policy treatment when Reject-specific absence is verified; an unrelated limited control cannot turn a paid alternative into an ordinary missing-Reject deduction. Known Options evidence remains projectable independently. Existing jurisdiction, consent-context and scoring policies still decide whether an absence supports a review finding or deduction; no scoring weights change.

Public reports, both authenticated report implementations, compact executive cards and PDF exports use canonical persisted states. Known controls retain their binary answers; unresolved A/R/O states share an inspection-level reason. API/JSON evidence retains the original typed uncertainty. Other lane findings remain available.

## Validation and release measurement

The frozen regression corpus includes all **26** non-no-go unknown records, all **37** Reject-observed assessments, three known absences and two no-go controls from the audited window. All 68 historical records must round-trip unchanged. The five reviewed phrases must classify correctly from their retained fields; Joybuy's historical document mismatch remains unknown. Retained replay cannot manufacture the new frame/coverage evidence needed to rewrite historical absences.

Browser fixtures cover scoped vendor roles, visible/accessible conflicts, negations, optional defaults, category visibility, unlabeled choices, overflow, frame failure and same-URL navigation. End-to-end tests cover observation, typed materialization, persistence, normalized concerns, policy, checklist/score, and presentation. Live registration remains independently verified by the existing action contracts.

After release, measure fresh ordinary scans using the canonical calibration contact/cooldown program. Exclude repeated owned canaries from the primary metric. Report unusable sessions separately. Target at least **98%** binary Reject coverage on usable actionable surfaces and **99%** precision of `not_observed`, with no known refusal-present false absences. These are acceptance targets, not results established by the local fixtures. Fresh production calibration requires a deployed scanner and WC01 projector; this implementation does not backfill stored reports or launch live scans.

## Cost

No new browser lane, invocation, screenshot, retry, wait window, model call, object write, capacity change or retention extension. The existing geometry read performs bounded additional bookkeeping and category reads. Replaying the 26 retained inventories for size only gives mean **604 bytes** of geometry metadata (maximum 1,043), plus about **472 bytes** of inspection metadata per assessment. At 100,000 scans/month and 30-day retention, a model with two geometry copies and four assessment/projection copies adds approximately **0.309 GB** before rare category-state payloads.

Expected incremental cost remains **below $0.10/month** at that volume, allowing for small category payloads and existing provisioned database capacity; this is an estimate, not measured billing. S3 charges depend on storage volume/class and request count ([AWS pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/s3/pricing/)); this change adds bytes to existing objects, without additional requests. No capacity increase is included. Any later approach reaching the repository's $1/month threshold needs separate approval.
