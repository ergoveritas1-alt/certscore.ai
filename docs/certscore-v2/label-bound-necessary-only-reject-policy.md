# Label-bound necessary-only Reject activation

On October 9, 2026, the owner approved bounded Reject activation for the reviewed necessary-only controls and Portuguese “Rejeitar,” with conservative incremental compute up to $110 per 100,000 affected scans. This approval adds no lane, retry, timeout extension, model call or deployment. Existing action windows, scan eligibility and exact-target authorization remain authoritative. The approval does not estimate the production occurrence rate.

`label_bound_necessary_only_reject.v1` permits “Strictly Necessary” and “Essential Cookies” only through the canonical OneTrust Reject v3 recipe and registered `#onetrust-reject-all-handler` control, within one visible `#onetrust-banner-sdk`. The canonical label classifier must recognize explicit, visible instructions naming that exact choice and its necessary-only/refusal effect. General category descriptions do not qualify. The control must be an enabled native button with `type=button` or an omitted type, outside every enclosing or associated form and without a `form` attribute, with unique actionable resolution and an authorized exact target. Explicit submit/reset buttons remain ineligible. Hidden instructions, another dialog, duplicate banners, malformed/unreadable context, source-label conflict and navigation drift fail closed. Bound visible text and the registered selector/policy provenance are retained in control-proof v2's optional typed `labelBoundNecessaryOnly` field; the contract validates the canonical label/context interpretation again. Legacy records without the new policy remain readable unchanged.

Generic necessary-only labels remain observation-only. This named-CMP activation specialization does not reuse the separate optional-off preference-save recipe and does not authorize entering a preference center. The last proof read rechecks scope/context before dispatch, and existing final target/cancellation guards remain in force.

The canonical label registry v12 separately recognizes exact “Rejeitar” as a direct Portuguese refusal label requiring consent context. Generic contextual matches retain their existing confidence and dispatch threshold. Informational, conflicting and transactional semantics remain vetoed. Both reviewed activation paths rebuild their bound proof at the final dispatch boundary. A guard veto remains unattempted: it emits no action-dispatched event, starts no after-action capture, and cannot be promoted by an unsolicited consent receipt.

Activation is separate from semantic registration. A completed click continues the existing bounded after-click capture. A complete, fresh retained refusal receipt is still required to assert confirmed refusal and to qualify post-refusal findings through typed evidence → normalized concerns → concern policy → unified findings/scoring. Incomplete OneTrust category receipts remain unconfirmed; missing categories are never guessed. No findings or scores are created by this activation proof alone.

Verification is local before any release: direct contract/label tests, positive and adversarial browser fixtures, existing A/R regressions, then ledger-selected fresh paired diagnostic visits. Local egress measurements do not establish AWS or population reliability. The incremental bounded proof text contributes under $0.10/month storage at 100,000 affected scans, within the approved $110 envelope; one-time central bookkeeping is estimated below $0.10.
