## Proposed resolution

Prioritize a versioned exact-endpoint Magnite reader that allocates bounded work to privacy declarations, identity carriers and the exact auction witness, while recording unrelated metadata coverage separately. Fully inspect reviewed privacy/GPP locations and reject duplicates/conflicts. Never skip a deep subtree and call the body complete. This is a proposed interpretation policy, not an implementation or permission to redefine the existing global 128-member/depth-8 guarantees.

A simpler alternative is an explicitly versioned Magnite-only structural allowance: evaluate 256 members and depth 10 offline, retaining the 32,768-byte cap and full duplicate/privacy traversal. The evidence stops at the old limits, so these candidate values are not proven sufficient. Unsupported field shapes may remain afterward. Do not apply larger limits globally.

First create sanitized representative impression/routing fixtures from the reviewed adapter, test adversarial duplicates and conflicting privacy/identity declarations, and measure worst-case parsing within the current scan deadline. Compare historical proof/policy reads before choosing an implementation. Only after those tests justify a specific change would a further scoped live validation be useful.

The evidence does not justify longer scans, more body bytes, network/CORS bypasses, altered crawler identity, broader vendor inference or rollout. Response, source/document/GPC and supported activity proof remain mandatory. Unread GPP remains unassessed.
