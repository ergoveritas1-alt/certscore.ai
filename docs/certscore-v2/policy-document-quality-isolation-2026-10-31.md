# CertScore.ai per-document policy quality — October 31, 2026

Legacy Article 13 candidate validation now assesses the candidate's own retained
document, independently of sibling documents. One shared disposition supplies
both accepted and discarded candidate tables. A challenge in another document
cannot remove a valid candidate; usable sibling text cannot supply a missing
document's quality. Existing document selection, topic validation, evidence
references and artifact verification remain authoritative.

This repairs candidate retention. Legacy candidates remain subject to the
existing canonical approval gate and do not become production-approved findings
merely because they survive this validation. The multilingual adapter already
checks document quality independently; its supported retained-section positives
continue through normalized concerns, policy and checklist when full text is
unavailable. No new full-text prerequisite is added to that approved path.

Aggregate extraction health and sufficient-coverage absence checks are separate.
The mixed challenge fixture retains valid candidates for retention, purposes
and rights while keeping extraction health limited and absence unproven. Tests
cover both document orders and evidence profiles, challenge and missing-text
negative controls, and unchanged canonical approval/checklist behavior.

The retained research sample of 50 originals and 75 exact text files showed no
historical exposure. Synthetic recoveries are not a measured fleet improvement.
The separate explicit document-URL mismatch proposal remains unimplemented.

Local implementation only. No scan, public visit, cloud query, model call,
deployment, flag change or historical rewrite. Bounded local quality processing
is conservatively estimated below $0.10/month at 100,000 scans; it replaces the
legacy aggregate candidate-quality pass and adds no capture, wait or retention.
