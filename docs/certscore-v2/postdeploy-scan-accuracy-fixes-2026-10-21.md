- Canonical privacy-surface classification recognizes combined Terms/Privacy
  labels in either order. Substantive, completely retained, target-owned combined
  bodies remain subject to independent body/role checks. Terms coverage and the
  canonical privacy role survive downstream projection without duplicate fetches.

  Newly materialized cookie availability consumes the admitted disclosure summary;
  raw inspection types, URL hints and provider preference pages cannot override it.
  Genuine observed target links remain factual surface evidence without a body.

  Exact target-domain scope in retained text may establish first-party attribution;
  a CDN URL, brand mention, vendor list or company relationship alone cannot.
- Policy capture checkpoints completed documents and their artifact references
  before sibling/optional work settles. A failed or expired later policy operation
  may publish that existing partial snapshot at the existing output barrier.
  Parent cancellation still terminates the scan; closed checkpoints cannot reopen
  publication. No negative-search completeness is invented for a partial snapshot.
- Passive CMP producer v20 replaces the old up-to-750ms DOMContentLoaded readiness
  checkpoint with a single up-to-100ms registry-bound read-only SDK checkpoint,
  scaled to at most 0.4 incremental GB-seconds. DOM-present/API-unready and empty
  OneTrust data are handled. Worker budgets, absolute deadlines, cleanup and lane
  counts remain unchanged. Options/Manage, SDK show/open, panel expansion, scrolling,
  preference navigation, form submission and choice mutation remain prohibited.
- Vendor resolver v10 adds narrow endpoint attribution for identity-bearing Google
  advertising-feature `/g/collect` and OpenX's page-context ESP endpoint. Same-request
  successful responses, original signal/session/document proof and canonical
  recipient deduplication remain mandatory. Criteo's plain sync-frame loader stays
  neutral. Advertising presence or a script download alone does not qualify for GPC.
- California assessment completion no longer treats broad sale/sharing topic
  presence as a completed clause assessment when the required language/scope
  proof is unavailable. That family stays partial, preserving unrelated verified
  evidence and the strict schema invariants.
- Cookie-purpose v5 adds only the vendor-documented `u` and `ts` cookies in the
  CreativeCDN domain context, with the existing RTB House entity ID. Generic `c`,
  first-party `__rtbh.lid`, `t2gt.tid`, and context-dependent `_dd_s` remain unknown
  without stronger source proof. Historical v2/v3/v4 classifications remain readable
  and retain their old purpose behavior. Reject presence uses the existing approved
  15/20/23/25/27/28/29/30 schedule and does not require refusal registration.

No customer deduction explanation or new customer disclaimer was added. The path
remains observed source -> typed retained assessment -> normalized concern ->
concern policy -> unified finding/checklist -> central scoring and shared surfaces.
