# Canonical non-essential presence after Reject

Owner-approved October 21, 2026, for CertScore.ai. This supersedes the active
15/5/0 measurement policy for new materializations. Historical assessments and
scores retain their original policy and amounts.

A completed, authorized Reject path with retained, canonically classified
non-essential cookies and/or services receives a family total of
15/20/23/25/27/28/29/30 points for 1–8+ distinct canonical non-essential entities,
capped at 30. Deduplicate each entity across all cookie and service evidence.
Unattributed classified non-essential observations support the initial 15-point
effect but cannot create additional entity increments. A verified contradiction
shares the same initial effect and does not stack with presence. A non-essential cookie in the post-click
snapshot qualifies even if its exact value was unchanged. A classified
non-essential service request beginning after the click qualifies without a
persistent identifier match, measurement exemption or refusal confirmation.
Observed non-essential service assets count as service presence; a script request
must not be described as proof of collection. Classified non-essential performance
or measurement services have no separate lower-tier exception under this policy.

Require the verified original packet SHA-256, exact authorized target/control and
frame provenance, a completed click, matching retained source projections and
bounded path completion. Unconfirmed paths require completed v2 after-click
capture with zero post-action request drops. Preserve approved registered-path
positive early completion; a positive finding does not require an extended
negative-observation window. Requests need strictly post-click redirect ancestry.
Cookie observations must come from the retained post-action snapshot and its
matching clock; pre-click cookies alone and unverified setter/header attempts do
not qualify. Unknown-purpose and essential evidence, vendor guesses, malformed
or missing sources, failed/cancelled/changed-target paths and incomplete captures
remain neutral. This does not convert missing evidence into an observed gap.

Presence does not establish active cookie use, browser write acceptance,
identifier transmission, successful refusal registration, a failure to honor
refusal, or a legal conclusion. Persist the factual, source-bound typed
assessment before normalized concern -> concern policy -> unified finding and
checklist -> central capped scoring. Customer reports show the resulting score
and factual findings/evidence, without point breakdowns or deduction explanations.
First-layer reportability and After Reject section visibility remain independent;
an action session must not override another lane's unknown or not-observed control.

Details retain each observation’s canonical entity attribution (or explicit unresolved
ownership), purpose, cookie name/request reference and milliseconds after the
Reject dispatch. Cookie times are snapshot-presence times, never inferred write
times. Service times are request-start times with verified post-click ancestry.
Report/API exports share these factual rows without scoring diagnostics.

Keep `after_reject_measurement_tiers.v1/v2`, GDPR/ePrivacy v20/v21, overall v10-v12
and full-site v9-v11 readable with their original interpretation. Source drift
invalidates retained assessments. Do not silently re-score stored reports,
regenerate a report on read, or apply a new assessment to an older score version.
The scoring registry displays the current entity schedule; legacy tier constants remain
only for reading/reproducing historical policy.

This is bounded interpretation of retained evidence. No new scan, browser work,
model/API call, lane, wait, timeout or retention change is introduced. Estimated
incremental bounded CPU/assessment metadata is below $0.50/month at 100,000 scans
under existing retention. Local implementation and evidence replay do not deploy
code or change historical production scores.
