# CertScore.ai supporting assessment highlights

Local implementation authorized October 29, 2026. No deployment, flags, live scans, historical rewrites or budget changes.

The shared narrative selector previously inherited priority-list bans for analytics-only California collection and sole non-discrimination omissions. Both could have eligible canonical score effects yet be categorically absent from highlights, even when little else was available. The new shared editorial policy permits qualified supporting observations below stronger findings without changing priority counts or treating score effects as eligibility evidence.

## Implementation

- `assessment-highlight-selection.ts` distinguishes priority, supporting and excluded canonical findings. Explicit low severity without a high-priority override uses the supporting tier in both views. Existing strong findings lead; observed collection precedes a sole wording omission within the supporting tier. The same brief and renderer serve EU and California.
- `regulatory-assessment-highlights.ts` supplies framework-relevant supporting observations to the writer while preserving the existing priority list/count. The collection-review identity now has explicit California catalog context, closing the generic-other-list route into EU selection.
- The renderer first retains stronger observations and their action. Supporting observations require complete facts and qualifications within the unchanged 500/1,000 character limits. Known concise variants may preserve the substantive stronger observation while leaving measurements in linked evidence; prose and qualifications are never clipped. No minimum summary count, raw-signal finding or generic unresolved-status task is added. Omitted supporting observations cannot lend their action to another finding.
- Existing collection overlap verification runs before analytics-only demotion, so allowing supporting analytics cannot resurrect an exact-source duplicate. Independent paths and mismatched source/session/document/recipient/request proof retain the previous protections.
- Priority eligibility, scoring policies and weights, normalized concerns, canonical packets, proof requirements, capture, budgets, persisted assessments and detailed evidence remain unchanged. Customer reports contain no point explanations.

The canonical rules are in [the shared highlights policy](../assessment-highlights-policy.md). This supersedes the earlier recommendation to keep these two finding classes entirely out of highlights; it does not relax other demotions or framework boundaries.
