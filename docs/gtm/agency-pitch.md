# CertScore Agency Pitch

## Why agencies should care
CertScore helps agencies review client websites faster and with more structure.

Instead of relying on scattered screenshots and ad hoc notes, agencies can:
- scan client sites across accessibility, privacy, and legal/disclosure basics
- generate structured reports with prioritized findings
- produce downloadable PDFs for handoff
- compare scans over time to catch regressions
- monitor multiple domains inside one organization

## Core positioning
CertScore is a practical first-pass scanner for client websites. It does not replace legal counsel or a deep manual audit, but it helps agencies identify obvious risk signals earlier and turn reviews into a repeatable service.

## Short call script
"We built CertScore for agencies that need a faster way to review client websites for accessibility, privacy, and disclosure-related risk signals. Instead of doing the same checks manually every time, you can run a scan, generate a structured report, share a PDF, and rerun it later to see what changed. It is a strong fit if you manage multiple sites and want a more repeatable reporting workflow."

## Good qualifying questions
- How are you reviewing client websites today?
- Do you already provide accessibility or compliance-related recommendations?
- How often do clients ask for a formal report or summary?
- Would repeat scans and regression tracking be useful for your workflow?
