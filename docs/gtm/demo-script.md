# CertScore Demo Script

## Goal
Deliver a clear 3-5 minute walkthrough that shows CertScore as a practical website risk signal scanner for SMBs, agencies, and developers.

## 1. Problem Statement
Start with:

"Most small businesses do not know what legal risk signals are sitting on their website until a client, agency, or attorney looks closely. Manual review is slow, inconsistent, and hard to repeat after the site changes."

Then position CertScore:

"CertScore gives you a fast first pass across accessibility, privacy, and disclosure basics so you can see what may need attention before problems compound."

## 2. Preview Scan
Show the homepage at `certscore.ai`.

Talk track:

"A visitor can enter a public website and get a quick preview scan. This is designed for fast triage, demos, and lead generation. It surfaces potential risk signals without forcing the user into a long setup flow."

Point out:
- the domain entry flow
- the quick preview state
- the preview findings and CTA to continue

## 3. Full Scan
Transition into the authenticated app.

Talk track:

"Once the user creates an account, CertScore runs the full workflow. That expands from a homepage preview into a deeper multi-page scan with saved history, plan-aware scan depth, and repeatable monitoring."

Show:
- dashboard
- domains page
- a domain detail page
- queued/running/completed scan status

## 4. Report Walkthrough
Open a completed report.

Talk track:

"The report is structured to answer four questions quickly: what risk was observed, which category is driving it, what should be fixed first, and what evidence was captured."

Highlight:
- overall score
- category scores
- exposure band
- executive summary
- top risk drivers
- findings grouped by category

## 5. PDF Export
Show PDF availability from the report page.

Talk track:

"Each completed scan can produce a downloadable PDF based on the same canonical report payload. That makes it easier to share findings internally, send a client-ready artifact, or keep a dated record of what was observed."

## 6. Regression Example
Show a domain or report with scan history.

Talk track:

"CertScore also compares each completed scan to the previous completed scan for the same domain. That lets teams see what is new, what was resolved, and whether the score improved or worsened over time."

Point out:
- new findings count
- resolved findings count
- score delta
- baseline messaging for first scans

## 7. Closing Pitch
Close with:

"CertScore is not a legal opinion or certification platform. It is a practical risk signal scanner that helps teams identify obvious issues earlier, prioritize remediation, and maintain documentation as the site evolves."

Suggested CTA:
- "If you want, I can run a sample scan flow on one of your sites."
- "If you manage multiple sites, I can show how the reporting and monitoring workflow fits agency operations."
