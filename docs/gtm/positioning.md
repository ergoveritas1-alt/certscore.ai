# CertScore Positioning

## Target Users
- SMB owners
- agencies
- WordPress and Shopify developers
- consultants and attorneys supporting website reviews

## Core Problem
Most website owners and service providers do not have a fast, repeatable way to identify obvious accessibility, privacy, and disclosure risk signals across public websites.

## Product Category
CertScore is a website legal risk signal scanner for public business websites.

## What CertScore does
CertScore scans public pages, surfaces observed issues, assigns deterministic scores, and generates structured reports that help teams prioritize remediation and maintain documentation over time.

## Key Differentiators
- scans accessibility, privacy, and legal/disclosure basics in one workflow
- deterministic scoring and structured findings
- report + PDF + history from the same canonical payload
- regression tracking for repeat monitoring
- agency-friendly client grouping and light branding support

## Comparison vs Manual Review
Manual review is often slower, less consistent, and harder to rerun after site changes. CertScore provides a faster first pass, structured evidence, and repeatable monitoring.

## Comparison vs Traditional Audits
Traditional audits can be deeper and more customized, but they are often slower and more expensive to repeat. CertScore is positioned as an earlier, faster, and more repeatable layer that helps teams identify what may need deeper review.

## Important posture
CertScore is not legal advice, not a legal certification platform, and not a replacement for counsel or specialized audits. It is designed to identify potential risk signals and support prioritization, documentation, and monitoring.
