# CertScore SMB Pitch

## Why small businesses should care
Most small businesses do not have time to manually review their website for accessibility, privacy, and disclosure gaps. CertScore helps them get a clearer first picture of what may be exposing the business.

CertScore helps SMBs:
- detect potential website legal risk signals earlier
- understand observed issues in plain English
- prioritize what to fix first
- keep documentation of ongoing review efforts

## Core positioning
CertScore is a website risk signal scanner for public sites. It is designed to help owners and operators understand what may deserve attention before they invest in deeper review or remediation.

## Short pitch
"If you are not sure whether your website is exposing the business to avoidable accessibility, privacy, or disclosure risk, CertScore gives you a fast first pass. It scans the site, shows the most important findings, explains them in plain English, and keeps a record over time as the site changes."

## Best-fit use cases
- local businesses with marketing sites
- ecommerce brands with tracking and policy concerns
- businesses that recently redesigned their site
- owners who want a clearer remediation priority list
