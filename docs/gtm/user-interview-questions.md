# CertScore User Interview Questions

## Goal
Use these prompts to learn why early users try CertScore, what they understand, what they trust, and what would make the product more valuable.

## Before the scan
- What made you decide to run a scan today?
- What were you hoping to learn from CertScore?
- Were you already concerned about accessibility, privacy, or website disclosures?

## During the experience
- Was the preview scan useful? Why or why not?
- What part of the flow felt most clear?
- What part of the flow felt confusing or incomplete?

## Report usefulness
- What part of the report was most useful?
- Which section felt least useful?
- Did the scores and risk drivers help you understand what to fix first?
- Did the findings feel credible and actionable?

## Product fit
- Would you use this for one site or multiple sites?
- Would this be more useful for you personally or for clients/internal stakeholders?
- Would you run this regularly after site changes?
- What would make this feel like a must-have tool instead of a nice-to-have?

## Trust and objections
- What made you trust or distrust the results?
- Was the "not legal advice" positioning clear?
- Did anything feel too technical, too vague, or too strong?

## Pricing and adoption
- Which plan feels closest to what you would actually need?
- What would need to be true for you to pay for this?
- What would stop you from adopting it right now?
