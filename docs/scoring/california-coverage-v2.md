Shared positive passage location also recognizes enumerated information and “only as long as necessary” retention wording without treating Canadian access rights as California rights.

Six families separately describe usable evidence and unresolved review:

- Privacy notice: target-owned governing text, categories, purposes and retention passages.
- California rights: distinct know, delete, correct and nondiscrimination clauses. Current v5 distinguishes general consumer wording from California wording and other-jurisdiction-only wording; retained general wording does not establish California adequacy.
- Request methods: retained request instructions or a verified named route. Discovery does not establish successful submission or response handling.
- Collection notice: category/purpose text matched to observed form fields or passive collection/activity points, with association retained separately. Ordinary privacy-policy links can supply matching text; a special notice title is not required. Unknown fields, incomplete association capture and missing text remain limited.
- Sale/sharing: explicit policy claims, independently of legal applicability. Qualified, conflicting and unlocated statements remain review. Canonically advertising-classified baseline activity alongside a no-sale/sharing claim requires role review; it is not automatically a legal contradiction.
- Choices: complete named-control inspection and, when triggered, verified bounded opt-out observation. A policy declaring no sale/sharing is retained as a claim, not independently verified activity.

California posture assumes CCPA/CPRA applies for the purpose of assessment. Actual business applicability remains undetermined. Location does not change the policy; it remains a property of the retained visit.
