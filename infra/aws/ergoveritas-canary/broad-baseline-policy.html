Synthetic privacy policy

Scope of this notice

This notice describes how the ErgoVeritas Canary Publisher handles personal data in this controlled scanner-validation environment. It applies to visits to the broad-baseline fixture, interactions with its consent controls, diagnostic requests generated by the fixture, and communications sent to the canary operator. The environment is synthetic, does not provide a consumer service, and is designed to create deterministic evidence for automated compliance-scanning review.

Controller and contact

ErgoVeritas Canary Publisher is the data controller for this fixture. Questions about this notice or the exercise of privacy rights may be directed to the privacy contact point at ergoveritas1@gmail.com. The privacy contact point coordinates data-protection questions for this controlled environment.

The designated data protection officer can be contacted through the same privacy contact point at ergoveritas1@gmail.com.

Categories of personal data

The fixture may process browser and device attributes, request metadata, cookie identifiers, approximate network information, consent-choice events, page interaction events, and any information a person voluntarily includes in a message to the operator. The fixture does not request payment details, government identifiers, health information, or account credentials.

Processing purposes and legal basis

We process service-request data to provide the requested service under contract, security telemetry to protect the fixture and diagnose abuse for a legitimate-interest purpose, and optional analytics only on the basis of consent. We also process communications to answer privacy questions and exercise requests, and we use diagnostic events to validate whether the CertScore scanner correctly recognizes the deliberately configured signals.

The lawful bases relied on are performance of a contract when a requested diagnostic service is provided, legitimate interests for security and controlled quality assurance, consent for optional analytics and embedded media, and compliance with a legal obligation when a valid request must be handled under applicable law. Where consent is the basis, it may be withdrawn through an available preference mechanism without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Named vendors and recipient categories

Named recipients include Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and YouTube for the synthetic analytics and embedded-media scenarios. Analytics providers process diagnostic measurement events, hosting providers serve the controlled pages, security providers help detect abuse, and professional advisers may receive information when necessary for the stated purposes. These recipients act as service providers, processors, or independent recipients according to the role documented for the relevant activity.

Privacy rights and complaints

Individuals may request access to their personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion or erasure, restriction of processing, portability, or objection to processing based on legitimate interests. They may also withdraw consent and ask for information about the recipients associated with their data. Requests may be sent to the privacy contact point identified above, and identity may be verified proportionately before a request is completed.

An individual also has the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority or local data-protection authority. Contacting the operator first is optional and does not limit the right to approach a supervisory authority.

Automated decision-making and profiling

This fixture does not use solely automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects or similarly significant effects for an individual. Automated scanner classification is used only to evaluate synthetic website signals, and its result does not determine access to a service, eligibility, employment, credit, insurance, or another consequential decision.

Cookies and storage

The controlled runtime uses _ga, _gid, _ga_CANARY, _fbp, _gcl_au, certscore_canary_storage, and certscore_canary_functional. Analytics identifiers support synthetic measurement, marketing identifiers support advertising-technology detection tests, and the functional identifier supports operation of the fixture. The consent surface is intended to describe these categories, even though the broad-baseline scenario deliberately activates optional technologies before a choice.

Security and accountability

The operator applies access controls, change review, controlled hosting, and monitoring appropriate to a public synthetic test environment. Scanner evidence is bounded to the diagnostic purpose, and the fixture avoids asking visitors to submit sensitive information. Suspected misuse or security questions may be reported to the privacy contact point.

Policy/runtime comparison

This policy says optional analytics is consent-based and embedded media is optional. The linked broad-baseline runtime intentionally loads those technologies before consent so the scanner can detect a controlled policy/runtime mismatch.
