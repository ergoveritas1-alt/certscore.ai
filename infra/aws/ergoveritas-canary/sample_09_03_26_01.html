A transparent consent-path test page

ErgoVeritas owns this synthetic page for CertScore scanner validation. It intentionally creates analytics activity before a choice and after both Accept and Reject so a real scan can demonstrate evidence capture and choice-path comparison. It is not a consumer service and does not involve an account or transaction.

Before a choice

A synthetic analytics cookie and request are created before the visitor chooses.

After Accept

The page records an affirmative choice and starts a bounded analytics and advertising comparison baseline.

After Reject

The page records a refusal but intentionally repeats the same non-essential activity, creating a Reject Path finding of interest.

What this sample is designed to show

Visible controls alone do not establish that a choice is honored. CertScore compares retained pre-choice evidence with separately isolated post-Accept and post-Refusal observations. Coverage limitations remain explicit, and the resulting report is an automated review aid—not legal advice, certification, or a compliance determination under GDPR/ePrivacy, CCPA/CPRA, or other law.
