Privacy Policy for the ErgoVeritas test 2 canary

ErgoVeritas is the owner of test2.html. This policy applies only to that owned scanner-validation page. It describes deliberately varied signals so CertScore can test evidence capture; it does not describe other ErgoVeritas products.

Information collected and why

On a fresh visit without GPC or a saved manual choice, the test page sets six first-party cookie identifiers and analytics/session storage. It requests analytics, advertising, session replay and product-measurement endpoints. With GPC, it sets five of those cookies and omits the advertising request. The purposes in this test are audience measurement, advertising signal classification, replay detection and product-event measurement. No account, purchase, form submission or authentication data is collected by the page.

Advertising, sale and sharing

The test sends an advertising request to Meta and measurement or replay requests to Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Hotjar, FullStory and Mixpanel before a choice. These are intentional signals for review of targeted advertising and possible sale or sharing. The test does not itself reach a legal conclusion about any individual request.

Global Privacy Control

When Global Privacy Control (GPC) is enabled in the browser, this canary omits the Meta advertising request and its _fbc test cookie. Five other optional requests and their test cookies remain. A complete paired scan can observe this partial reduction; an incomplete comparison cannot establish the site's response. A saved manual Do Not Sell/Share choice turns off all future optional test activity in this browser.

Opt-out and cookie choices

Visitors can use Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information, Your Privacy Choices, or Cookie Settings. Saving any of these test choices suppresses optional requests on later visits in that browser and removes the first-party test cookies. The scanner observes links and notice passages; it does not verify these manual actions in an ordinary passive scan.

Retention and privacy rights

First-party test cookies expire after 15 minutes and saved manual-choice markers after 24 hours. This page makes no statement about providers' separate retention. The canary does not process real requests to access, delete, correct, or obtain a copy of personal information; the test controls should not be treated as such a process.
