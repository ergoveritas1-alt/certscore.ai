Cookie choices

Choose whether this site may use optional analytics and advertising technologies. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and optional activity should stop unless you accept.

Cookie options

Necessary cookies are always active. Analytics and advertising are separate optional purposes and are off by default.

Regulatory audit scenario

Finding of interest: the first-layer controls are balanced and Reject is honored after the choice, but Google Analytics already accessed storage and made a request before the visitor could choose.

This separates two questions auditors test independently: whether the interface offers a genuine choice, and whether non-essential technologies waited for that choice. A later clean Reject does not cure earlier pre-consent activity.

Expected boundaries

  • Both named OneTrust actions resolve uniquely and confirm through a changed TCF/CMP state.
  • Accept and Reject are equally prominent first-layer actions, with separate optional purposes available under Manage options.
  • Passive capture sees a pre-choice _ga cookie and Google Analytics request that should remain a pre-consent timing concern.
  • A request initiated before semantic registration must be retained as in-flight and excluded from post-action findings.
  • Accept produces delayed analytics and advertising requests plus _ga and _gid analytics writes only after semantic registration.
  • Reject clears the baseline analytics cookie before refusal registration and produces no direct post-refusal activity.
  • The two fresh-context outcomes are therefore distinguishable.
