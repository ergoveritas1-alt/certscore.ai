Cookie choices

Choose whether this site may use optional analytics and advertising technologies. The interface presents balanced choices, but this audit fixture deliberately fails to honor them consistently.

Cookie options

Necessary cookies are always active. Analytics and advertising are presented as separate optional purposes and are off by default.

Regulatory audit scenario

Findings of interest: non-essential analytics operates before any choice, the CMP records Accept while its retained TCF purpose state still says no, and Reject is followed by the same analytics and advertising identities as Accept.

This demonstrates why an auditor must test behavior and retained consent state rather than treating the presence of Accept, Reject, and Manage options as proof that choices are effective.

Expected boundaries

  • Accept confirms through the changed canonical CMP cookie, not through the contradictory TCF state.
  • Passive capture sees a pre-choice _ga cookie and Google Analytics request.
  • The retained post-Accept TCF state denies every configured purpose and should create a consent-state contradiction review signal.
  • Reject confirms through the denied TCF state.
  • Both actions directly write the same exact _ga and _gid identities and request the same analytics and advertising endpoints after registration.
  • The comparison should highlight indistinguishable outcomes and preserve the post-Reject activity issue as the actionable failure.
