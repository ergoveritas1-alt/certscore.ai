Deliberate evidence scenarios

  • Accept, Reject, and Options are all visible. Accept has a dark, wider button; Reject has a pale border.
  • Category controls are available in Options; individual service controls are absent.
  • Both policies are accessible before interacting with the banner.
  • A timestamped, versioned receipt records each choice locally. This does not demonstrate a server audit trail.
  • Analytics and advertising cookies are set before a choice. Confirmed Reject is followed by another analytics write and request.
  • The 400-day Accept period and one-day Reject period are deliberately unequal.
  • example_insights_id is declared but never set; _fbp and post-choice _gid are not declared in the inventory.
  • The declared _ga duration is one day, while the runtime writes a 30-day cookie.

These are fixture expectations for evidence review. Scanner results must come from the captured visit; an unmeasured criterion remains Not assessed.
